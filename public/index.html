My work

Right now I’m studying geometric flows under Mat Langford at ANU’s Mathematical Sciences Institute. I’m working on Ricci flow and a related conformal flow. (For the geometry fans out there, it’s the flow $\partial_t g_t = -R^\alpha g_t$; for now in 2 dimensions. The nonlinearity of this equation demands some interesting techniques when studying the behavior of solutions to this flow.)

See here for a 3-part series of accessible articles on research in curvature flows. See Anthony Carapetis’s page for an interactive visualization of the curve shortening flow, which is perhaps the simplest example of a curvature flow.

I also am generally involved in one or more programming or data analysis projects, for fun and profit, as well as reasons of employability (on that note, I’m looking for a postdoc position or a job!). In this direction, my interests tend towards game theory, algorithm design, and time-series analysis.
