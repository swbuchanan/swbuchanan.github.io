## My work

Right now I'm studying geometric flows under Mat Langford at ANU's Mathematical Sciences Institute.
I'm working on Ricci flow and a related conformal flow.
(For the geometry fans out there, it's the flow $\partial_t g_t = -R^\alpha g_t$; for now in 2 dimensions.
The nonlinearity of this equation demands some interesting techniques when studying the behavior of solutions to this flow.)

[See here for a 3-part series of accessible articles on research in curvature flows.]({{< ref "/posts/2023-01-03-my-research-part-1.md" >}})
See [Anthony Carapetis&#39;s page](https://a.carapetis.com/csf/) for an interactive visualization of the curve shortening flow, which is perhaps the simplest example of a curvature flow.

I also am generally involved in one or more programming or data analysis projects, for fun and profit, as well as reasons of employability (on that note, I'm [looking for a postdoc position or a job!](./pdfs/Resume.pdf)).
In this direction, my interests (besides mathematics) tend towards game theory, algorithm design, and data analysis.
